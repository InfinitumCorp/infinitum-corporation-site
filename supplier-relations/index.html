Supplier Relations

Partner with Infinitum Corporation to supply the components, services and technology behind our products and client projects.

01What we buy

  • Electronics, radios, sensors and components for ÆTHER™ Nodes and our autonomous systems.
  • Manufacturing, assembly and ruggedization services.
  • Cloud, data-center and network infrastructure.
  • Software, security tools and specialist engineering services.
  • Logistics, facilities and professional services that support client projects.

02Becoming a supplier

Send an introduction to info@infinitumcorporation.ai with your company name, location, ownership details, the products or services you offer, relevant certifications, and a point of contact. We will reach out when your offering matches a current or upcoming need.

03What we expect

  • Ethics: honest dealing, no bribery or kickbacks, and no conflicts of interest.
  • Labor and human rights: no forced or child labor, and safe, fair working conditions.
  • Quality: products and services that meet agreed specifications, with traceable sourcing and no counterfeit parts.
  • Security: protection of Infinitum and client information, prompt reporting of security incidents, and, for defense work, compliance with applicable DFARS and CMMC requirements.
  • Trade compliance: compliance with U.S. export-control (ITAR and EAR) and sanctions laws, and with sourcing restrictions on covered foreign components.
  • Sustainability: compliance with environmental laws and responsible use of materials.

04Government and defense work

Suppliers supporting government or military programs may need to meet additional contract requirements, including flow-down clauses, security clearances for personnel, facility security requirements, and U.S.-person restrictions on access to controlled technical data.

05Invoices and contact

Send invoices to the contact named in your purchase order, quoting the purchase order number. For all other supplier questions, contact info@infinitumcorporation.ai or write to Infinitum Corporation, 1629 K Street NW, Suite 300, Washington, D.C.
