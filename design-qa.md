# One-page redesign

Applied design guidance: https://github.com/anthropics/skills/blob/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md

Design decisions: white (#ffffff), ink (#111820), secondary text (#52606b), rules (#d6dce1), interactive blue (#2454dd). Large restrained sans-serif typography; text-only hero; one continuous navigation path. Broad hero gives way to a compact service explorer, expandable product rows, filterable organization portfolio and direct contact section. Interaction-driven motion, one hero entrance, reduced-motion support and a pause button.

Main navigation uses section anchors. /capabilities and /products redirect to their homepage sections. Detailed product notes and the full research/client directory remain accessible.

Validation: 26 HTML pages, all internal links/assets/anchors, all 10 original service areas and 16 products. Desktop and 390px mobile reviewed; service tabs tested by pointer and keyboard; product category filters; CCW search; government-sector filtering (7 results); no console errors observed. Vercel preview redirect verified.
