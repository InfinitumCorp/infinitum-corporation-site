Digital assets & the Infinitum Nexus™

Use the product.
Earn from it.

Every Infinitum product is built to run on $Firmament Network™ and to carry its own token. Use a product, choose what you contribute, and share in the revenue it creates.

  1. 01

    Use

    Work, create or connect with any Infinitum product.

  2. 02

    Contribute

    Choose which data leaves your device. Private information is filtered before anything is shared.

  3. 03

    We process & license

    Infinitum refines consented data into licensable assets and sells access to verified buyers.

  4. 04

    Earn

    Your share of the revenue is paid in that product's token, settles through $FIRM™ and lands in $FIRM Wallet™.

One nexus. Every product connected.

Each product token pairs with $FIRM™, the $Firmament Network™ token, so value moves between products without leaving the ecosystem. Earn $HRVST from field observations and spend it on Cumulus://Cloud™ storage, or convert through $FIRM™ to USDC.

  • SolanaNexus settlement and the deepest liquidity
  • SolanaEnterprise, cloud, AI and intelligence products
  • SolanaEvery Infinitum token, $FIRM included, is issued and traded here. No bridges.

One wallet for every token

$FIRM Wallet™

Every Infinitum token lives in $FIRM Wallet™: a standard self-custody wallet for Solana, with a built-in decentralized exchange for swapping any product token through $FIRM™ or USDC.
