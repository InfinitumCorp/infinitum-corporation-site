E(O)N MIL™

E(O)N MIL™ is Infinitum's wearable electronic-warfare and counter-drone platform, built only for authorized military and defense organizations. It turns every person in the field into a spectrum sensor, gives them protection against jamming, interception and detection, and defends them against hostile drones.

Electronic support · Electronic protection · Counter-drone · Signature control · Spectrum picture

OVERVIEW
Military use only

The electromagnetic spectrum is contested everywhere troops operate. E(O)N MIL™ is worn on the body, listens across the bands around the wearer, recognizes emitters and interference with on-device AI, and keeps the wearer's own communications working and hard to find. When a hostile drone approaches, it warns the wearer, points to it and can disrupt its control, video and navigation links.

Each E(O)N MIL™ feeds a shared spectrum picture through ODIN°™, so a team of wearers becomes a distributed sensing network that locates emitters, maps jamming and warns everyone at once.

There is no commercial edition. E(O)N MIL™ is sold only to authorized military and defense organizations as part of ODIN°™, runs on closed, accredited infrastructure and sits outside the token economy.

01 / HOW IT WORKS

Sense. Protect. Share.

  1. 01 / SENSE

    Listen to the spectrum

    Wideband receivers scan around the wearer, and on-device AI classifies emitters, drone links and interference

  2. 02 / PROTECT

    Keep links alive

    Detects jamming and spoofing, then hops, reroutes or falls back to the clearest path automatically

  3. 03 / CONCEAL

    Control the signature

    Manages the wearer's own emissions to lower the chance of detection and direction finding

  4. 04 / SHARE

    Build the picture

    Sends detections to >_ARGUS MIL™ and ODIN°™, where many wearers locate emitters together

02 / CAPABILITIES

Electronic warfare, on the body.

  • Electronic support

    Detects, classifies and logs radio emitters, radar and drone control links, with alerts by tone, vibration or voice.

  • Electronic protection

    Anti-jam and anti-spoofing for voice, data and navigation, with automatic frequency agility and path switching.

  • Signature control

    Emission control profiles, low-probability-of-intercept modes and a live indicator of how visible the wearer is.

  • Counter-drone

    Detects, identifies and tracks hostile drones by their control, video and navigation signals, warns the wearer and the unit with a bearing, and can disrupt those links so the drone loses control or turns back.

  • Cooperative geolocation

    Several E(O)N MIL™ units work together to estimate where an emitter is, and share the result on the common picture.

  • Device integrity

    Checks every connected device for rooting or jailbreaking, tampering and emulators, and shows a device-health score.

  • Resilient navigation

    Detects GNSS jamming and spoofing and keeps position with inertial and assured-navigation fallbacks.

03 / COUNTER-DRONE

Every operator, a drone shield.

Small drones are now the most common threat a soldier sees overhead. E(O)N MIL™ gives each wearer their own counter-drone layer, and links them so the whole unit defends together.

  1. 01 / DETECT

    Hear it first

    Picks up drone control, video and navigation signals before the drone is in sight

  2. 02 / IDENTIFY

    Know what it is

    On-device AI recognizes the drone type and separates friendly from hostile

  3. 03 / LOCATE

    Point to it

    Gives a bearing, and with several wearers a position on the shared picture

  4. 04 / DEFEAT

    Break the link

    Disrupts the drone's control, video or navigation, or hands it to AZRAIL MIL™ interceptors

Counter-drone functions follow the customer's rules of engagement and applicable law, and protect friendly drones and communications.

04 / HARDWARE

Built to be worn.

  • Wearable unit

    Body-worn receiver and processor with an on-device AI accelerator, sealed against water and dust and sized for a plate carrier.

  • Wrist display

    Glanceable threat warnings, spectrum status and hands-free voice control from SCALVS.ai MIL™.

  • Secure core

    A hardware secure element holds the wearer's credentials and keys, with secure boot and remote zeroization.

  • Power

    Swappable batteries and AI power management that matches scanning to the mission and the wearer's day.
