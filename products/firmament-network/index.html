$Firmament Network™

$Firmament Network™ is the trust layer of the Infinitum ecosystem: one blockchain for identity, provenance, licensing, payments and audit across every product.

OVERVIEW
Ecosystem blockchain

Instead of building a separate blockchain for every product, every Infinitum product shares Firmament. Each keeps its own token, paired with $FIRM™, while identity, proof and payments work the same way everywhere.

Firmament is AI-guarded: models review smart contracts before deployment, watch transactions for anomalies and flag risks in real time. Sensitive data stays off-chain; only proofs and fingerprints are recorded.
