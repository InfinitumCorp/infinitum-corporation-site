Nøde™

Nøde™ is Infinitum's community connectivity network: free Wi-Fi for everyone, carried by participating hosts and dedicated Nøde™ routers.

Free for everyone. Growing location by location.

OVERVIEW
Pilot · in development

Nøde™ turns venues, homes and phones into one shared network. Fixed gateways bring the internet in; participating hosts and Nøde™ routers carry it further; anyone nearby connects for free.

Phones extend the network, but they cannot create internet access on their own. Every Nøde™ connection reaches the internet through a real broadband, cellular or satellite uplink, which is why Nøde™ starts with fixed gateways and grows from there.

01 / FOR EVERY TIER

Personal, business, government and military.

One network design scales from a single home to a whole base. Every Nøde™ network runs on the same app, controls and privacy rules.

  • Civilian

    Homes and apartments: free Wi-Fi nearby, and the option to share your own connection on your own terms.

  • Business

    Shops, office buildings, apartment complexes, hotels and venues: free guest Wi-Fi for visitors and residents.

  • Government

    Parks, public buildings, libraries, campuses and transit stops: free public Wi-Fi for whole neighborhoods.

  • Military

    Bases, base housing and training areas: Nøde™ networks for authorized defense organizations.

02 / THE NØDE™ APP

One app. Three ways to take part.

  • User

    Find a nearby Nøde™ hotspot and connect for free. Sign in once with your Cumulus://ID™.

  • Host

    Share part of an internet connection you already have through an approved hotspot setup, on your terms.

  • Local peer

    Exchange Nøde™ messages and files with phones nearby, even where there is no internet.

03 / HOSTS STAY IN CONTROL

Share what you choose. Stop when you want.

  • Off until you turn it on

    Sharing starts only when you switch it on. Using free Nøde™ Wi-Fi never signs you up to host, and never spends your cellular allowance.

  • Your limits

    Set bandwidth caps and daily data limits for what you share.

  • Battery and heat cutoffs

    Hosting pauses automatically when your battery runs low or your phone gets too warm.

  • Schedule and stop

    Share only at the times you pick, and stop instantly with one button.

  • Guests kept separate

    Guest traffic stays apart from your private network and your own devices.

04 / NØDE™ HARDWARE

Routers built for the network.

Nøde™ starts on existing compatible routers, then moves to Infinitum-built hardware. The routers form the mesh between themselves; phones connect to ordinary Wi-Fi.

  • Nøde™ Home

    Shares a controlled portion of a household's broadband.

  • Nøde™ Business

    Free guest Wi-Fi for shops, apartment buildings and venues.

  • Nøde™ Relay

    Extends coverage between fixed points of the network.

  • Nøde™ Gateway

    Connects a neighborhood network to a broadband or cellular uplink.

Fixed hardware brings steady power, better antennas, predictable coverage and managed updates. Several gateways in one area mean no single host keeps everyone online.

05 / HOW FREE IS PAID FOR

Funded by partners and aggregated insight.

Free basic access is funded by venues, sponsors and aggregated network analytics. Taking part in personal research is a separate choice you make yourself.

  • Connectivity reports

    Aggregated speed, latency, uptime and congestion, for network planners, internet providers and property operators.

  • Venue activity reports

    Group-level visit counts and busy periods, for retailers and property managers.

  • Consumer research

    Surveys and declared preferences, only from people who agree to each study.

  • Campaign measurement

    Aggregated reach and results for advertisers and venues.

06 / PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Insight from groups, never from you alone.

  • Identity kept apart

    Your account is stored separately from network analytics.

  • Only groups

    Small groups are suppressed, and places and times are reported in broad areas and windows.

  • No re-identification

    Every data license forbids buyers from trying to identify anyone.

  • Never for sale

    Private messages, passwords, payment details, precise movement histories and sensitive personal profiles are never sold.

  • Permission on record

    Every permission and every data license is recorded on $Firmament Network™, so each use can be traced back to a choice.

07 / ROLLOUT

One neighborhood at a time.

  • 1. Pilot area

    A building, apartment complex, campus, park, shopping district or base.

  • 2. Fixed gateways

    Partner venues whose internet service allows public sharing.

  • 3. The Nøde™ app

    Hotspot discovery, sign-in, permission controls and host settings.

  • 4. Phone hosting

    Switched on only for phones and plans we have tested.

  • 5. Nøde™ hardware

    Rolled out once coverage and costs are measured in the field.

Host a gateway.
