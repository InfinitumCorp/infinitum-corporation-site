DAEMON™

DAEMON™ is Infinitum's drone series: CHERUBIM™ for quick-reaction overwatch and close-quarters reconnaissance, SERAPHIM™ for long-endurance reconnaissance and patrol, and AZRAIL MIL™ for counter-drone defense, all linked live to METATRON¹⁰⁰™ and ODIN°™.

OVERVIEW
Military use only

Every DAEMON™ aircraft is built to be fielded in numbers: simple to make, quick to launch without a runway, and flown by onboard AI that keeps working when GPS and links are jammed. People stay in command of every mission.

The whole series shares one core: the same autonomy computer, the same mesh radio and the same flight record on $Firmament Network™. What one aircraft sees, and every flight it makes, appears in METATRON¹⁰⁰™ or, for authorized military users, ODIN°™.

There is no consumer or commercial edition. DAEMON™ is sold only to authorized military and defense organizations as DAEMON MIL™.

01 / THE SERIES

One series, three classes.

  • CHERUBIM™

    Quick reaction, overwatch and close quarters. Three models: CHERUBIM 1™ launches from a base dock the moment an alert comes in, CHERUBIM 2™ stands tethered overwatch for hours, and CHERUBIM 3™ flies inside buildings ahead of a team. Military edition.

  • SERAPHIM™

    Long-endurance search and patrol. A fixed-wing aircraft that takes off vertically from a parking lot or ship deck and flies for up to ten hours over areas far larger than any patrol can cover. Military edition.

  • AZRAIL MIL™

    Counter-drone defense. An autonomous aircraft that patrols over a unit and stops hostile drones and swarms by direct impact or net capture, with no explosive and an operator approving every engagement. Military only.

04 / BUILT IN VOLUME

Made in numbers.

  • Distributed production

    DAEMON™ is built in small, modular production cells that can be set up close to where aircraft are needed, with partners and allied nations.

  • Propulsion in house

    Infinitum designs and builds the electric drives, hybrid engines and power systems, so supply never waits on a single outside vendor.

  • Common core

    One autonomy computer, one mesh radio and one software stack across the series, so training, spares and updates carry over.

  • Low unit cost

    Simple airframes and shared parts keep each aircraft affordable enough to field in large numbers.

  • Mission records

    Every flight, engagement approval and maintenance event is fingerprinted on $Firmament Network™ for accountability.

  • Fleet ready

    Manage hundreds of aircraft and their operators together from METATRON¹⁰⁰™, or from ODIN°™ for military fleets.
