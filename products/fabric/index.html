OVERVIEW
Communications platform

Your communications have value. FabriC++™ keeps them private with end-to-end encryption, and makes them smarter with an assistant that summarizes threads, translates calls live and drafts replies, on your device.

If you choose, FabriC++™ can license authorized, aggregated usage data to approved buyers and share 85% of eligible net revenue with participating users in $FAB, paid straight to $FIRM Wallet™.

02 / WHAT MAKES IT SMARTER

Intelligence built in.

  • End-to-end encrypted

    Messages, voice, video and files, protected between devices.

  • AI that stays private

    On-device summaries, live call translation and smart replies, without sending your conversations to the cloud.

  • One identity

    Sign in with your Cumulus://ID™ (CID) and reach anyone in the ecosystem at their internal address, like @alex.veil.

  • Earn from your data

    Opt in by purpose, see every sale, and receive your share in $FAB in $FIRM Wallet™.

  • Logged to your CRM

    With permission, calls and messages with customers appear in Spectre™ automatically.

  • Burner numbers

    Add disposable numbers that keep your real number private. Add-ons come in bundles of 1, 2 or 5, renewed monthly or yearly.

  • Works everywhere

    Over the internet, Wi-Fi or FabriC++™ mesh when there is no network at all.

03 / HOW REVENUE IS SHARED

A worked example.

Eligible net brokerage revenue$100,000
User reward pool (85%)$85,000
Infinitum share (15%)$15,000

Average allocation$8.50 per user
