OVERVIEW
Infinitum cloud platform

Cumulus combines the simplicity of a personal drive with the power of a full cloud platform. Back up your photos, or deploy virtual machines, databases and AI workloads, all from one Cumulus://ID™ (CID) account.

It is also the cloud every Infinitum product runs on, and it is smart by default: AI organizes your files, forecasts your costs and right-sizes your infrastructure automatically.

CUMULUS://ID / CID

One account for everything Infinitum.

Cumulus://ID™, or CID, is your Infinitum account. Create it once and it signs you in to every Infinitum product on every device, and keeps your storage, mail, wallet, agents and settings together in one place.

  • One sign-in

    Every Infinitum app, site and device, from VEIL</>™ and FabriC++™ to #THRONE™. Passkeys, security keys, multifactor authentication and session revocation come standard.

  • Everything in one account

    15 GB of storage shared by Cumulus Drive™ and Cumulus://Mail™, your alex@cumuluscloud.app address and aliases, a $FIRM Wallet™ account, SCALVS.ai™ and :\ARCHITECT\™ Free access and your METATRON¹⁰⁰™ dashboard.

  • One permissions dashboard

    See which product can use what, grant access app by app and revoke it at any time. Every grant is recorded on $Firmament Network™.

  • Memory that follows you

    The Nexus Context Vault™ lets what one product learns help the others, tied to your CID and only with your permission.

  • Teams and organizations

    Business and enterprise CIDs with admin controls, single sign-on federation and per-user Cumulus storage.

  • Private by design

    Anonymous browsing in VEIL</>™ never needs a CID, and your CID is never sold. Military ODIN°™ accounts are issued separately, outside consumer accounts.

CUMULUS://MAIL

Email that comes with your cloud.

Cumulus://Mail™ is the email included with Cumulus://Cloud™. Every Cumulus://ID™ (CID) comes with a free address like alex@cumuluscloud.app, using the same storage as Cumulus Drive™.

  • Your address

    A permanent alex@cumuluscloud.app address, disposable and permanent aliases, and your own custom domains on paid plans.

  • Private by default

    Every mailbox is encrypted at rest and mail between Cumulus://Mail™ accounts is end-to-end encrypted. Cumulus://Mail™ is never scanned for ads and never sold.

  • AI built in

    SCALVS.ai™ summarizes long threads, drafts replies and flags what needs you. :\ARCHITECT\™ turns an email into a finished task.

  • Contacts and calendar

    Encrypted contacts and calendar that sync across devices, and share with +SPCTR™ (Spectre) only when you allow it.

  • Everywhere you work

    On the web, desktop and mobile, built into VEIL</>™ and #THRONE™, and in any email app through IMAP and SMTP.

  • Room to grow

    Free accounts share 15 GB with Cumulus Drive™. Personal, Pro and Business plans add storage, custom domains and admin controls.
