>_ARGUS MIL™

>_ARGUS MIL™ is Infinitum's AI sensor-fusion system for authorized military and defense organizations: the Observe layer of ODIN°™, turning every drone, sensor and field feed into one live picture.

Sensor fusion · Detection and tracking · Predictive alerts · Chain of custody

OVERVIEW
Military use only

Most sensor systems show raw feeds. >_ARGUS MIL™ understands them. It fuses imagery, telemetry, geospatial data and signals from DAEMON MIL™, POLLEN MIL™, SLUG MIL™, ORBIS MIL™, [HRVST] MIL™, E(O)N MIL™ and any other authorized source, then uses AI to detect, track and explain events as they happen.

There is no commercial edition. >_ARGUS MIL™ is sold only to authorized military and defense organizations as part of ODIN°™, runs on closed, accredited infrastructure and sits outside the token economy. Outside defense, cameras and sensors appear in METATRON¹⁰⁰™.

02 / WHAT MAKES IT SMARTER

Intelligence built in.

  • Live common picture

    One map with every asset, track, alert and feed, shared instantly with everyone cleared to see it.

  • AI detection & tracking

    Computer vision identifies and follows objects and events across sensors and aircraft, and hands tracks off between them automatically.

  • Ask the scene

    “What changed at the north gate in the last hour?” Ask in plain language and get the answer with the footage that proves it.

  • Supports air defense

    Unidentified aircraft and drones are tracked and shared with air-defense operators, who decide every response.

  • Evidence on record

    Every capture is fingerprinted on Firmament Network MIL™, giving each image and track a verifiable chain of custody.

  • Works at the edge

    Runs on #THRONE MIL™ hardware close to the sensors, and keeps working over the ÆTHER MIL™ mesh when links are jammed or down.
