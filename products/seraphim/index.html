SERAPHIM™

SERAPHIM™ is a new class of long-endurance aircraft: it takes off vertically from a parking lot or a ship deck, then flies like a plane for up to ten hours, searching and watching areas far larger than any patrol can cover. Part of the DAEMON™ drone series.

Wide-area search · All-day endurance · Vertical takeoff

OVERVIEW
Military use only

Some searches cover hundreds of square kilometers. SERAPHIM™ covers them in one flight: it launches without a runway, cruises quietly at altitude and uses onboard AI to spot people, vehicles and vessels, sending what it finds straight to the teams on the ground.

It follows a suspect vehicle from high above instead of a high-speed chase, finds a missing hiker before nightfall and maps a disaster zone while roads are still closed.

There is no consumer or commercial edition. SERAPHIM™ is sold only to authorized military and defense organizations as SERAPHIM MIL™.
