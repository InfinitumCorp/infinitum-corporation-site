02 / BROWSER TECHNOLOGY

Real Tor privacy, not a proxy.

VEIL</>™ is built on a Tor Browser-derived engine, not an ordinary browser pointed at a Tor proxy. It keeps every core Tor defense and adds the features people expect from a modern browser.

  • Tor protections

    Multi-relay routing, fingerprint resistance, first-party isolation, separated session cookies, DNS and WebRTC leak protection, and New Identity and New Circuit controls.

  • Secure by default

    HTTPS-first connections, authenticated onion services, process isolation and sandboxing, and automatic signed security updates.

  • Tracker and ad blocking

    Trackers and ads are blocked at the DNS level, before they load, and VEIL</>™ warns you when a connection is not safe.

  • A familiar browser

    Tabs and tab groups, bookmarks, secure downloads, a built-in document viewer, a custom home dashboard and an app launcher.

  • Your keys and passwords

    Built-in password and passkey manager, encrypted bookmarks with optional sync, offline content, keyboard shortcuts and accessibility controls.

Desktop editions use the Firefox ESR and Tor Browser technology family. Android uses a privacy-focused Gecko engine. iOS has its own edition built around Apple's browser-engine rules.

03 / TWO WORKSPACES

Anonymous when you want it. Signed in when you need it.

  • Anonymous workspace

    Tor-first browsing with no account, no AI integrations, no wallet and no cloud sync. Nothing links your browsing to you.

  • Authenticated workspace

    Your Cumulus://ID™ (CID), Cumulus://Mail™, $FIRM Wallet™, SCALVS.ai™, :\ARCHITECT\™, Cumulus://Cloud™ and other Infinitum services.

You can switch between them at any time, but identifying information never moves silently from one to the other. The signed-in workspace can also run over Tor where compatible, though a signed-in account is never described as anonymous.

04 / CID & CUMULUS://MAIL

One identity. A real inbox.

Anonymous browsing never needs an account. When you choose to create one, a single activation sets up your Cumulus://ID™ (CID), the one account behind every Infinitum product.

  • CID

    Your Infinitum account. One sign-on across Infinitum products with per-app permissions. Supports passkeys, hardware-backed sign-in, security keys, optional multifactor authentication, session revocation and organizational identity federation.

  • Created for you

    A unique username, a Cumulus://Mail™ address, private aliases, encrypted Cumulus://Cloud™ storage, a $FIRM Wallet™ account, SCALVS.ai™ and :\ARCHITECT\ Free™ access and a VEIL Rewards account.

  • Cumulus://Mail™

    The email that comes with Cumulus://Cloud™, built into VEIL</>™: a working address like alex@cumuluscloud.app, with disposable and permanent aliases, encrypted storage, custom domains, encrypted contacts and calendar, and anti-abuse protection.

  • Internal address

    An ecosystem identity such as @alex.veil for messaging and lookup across Infinitum, separate from your internet email address.

Cumulus://Mail™ encrypts every mailbox at rest and offers end-to-end encryption between compatible accounts. Mail sent to outside providers is protected in transit, and we say so plainly rather than calling it end-to-end.

05 / NATIVE AI

Intelligence and orchestration, preinstalled.

  • SCALVS.ai™ · Intelligence

    The assistant in your sidebar: chat, research, summaries, writing, coding help, page analysis, access to files you allow, and personal AI agents.

  • :\ARCHITECT\™ · Orchestration

    Workflow automation and multi-agent coordination: scheduling, multi-step tasks, approved API integrations, run history and human approval checkpoints.

Clear AI boundaries. Neither assistant can quietly look into anonymous Tor sessions, read private email, extract keys, open encrypted files, move money, export personal data or install components. Each of those needs your explicit permission and approval. AI runs outside the anonymous browsing engine so it never weakens Tor's fingerprint protection.

06 / $FIRM™ WALLET

The only wallet you need.

$FIRM Wallet™ is built into VEIL</>™. There is no separate VEIL wallet or rewards wallet: $VEIL, $FIRM™ and every other Infinitum token live in one place.

  • What it does

    Receives your $VEIL rewards, holds every ecosystem token, sends and receives assets, swaps tokens, shows your history and signs transactions you approve.

  • Self-custody

    Private keys are created and kept on your device, in hardware-backed storage where available. Infinitum never holds your unencrypted keys.

  • No surprises

    Every transaction shows the network fee, destination address and permissions before you confirm.

  • Off in anonymous mode

    The wallet stays disabled in the anonymous workspace unless you deliberately leave it through a clearly labeled action.

10 / SECURITY

Defense-oriented by design.

  • Zero trust

    Every user, device and request is verified before it reaches a protected resource.

  • Defense in depth

    Controls across browser processes, apps, operating systems, APIs and infrastructure.

  • Encryption & keys

    Independently reviewed encryption for private communications and secure key storage and signing.

  • Isolation

    Anonymous, signed-in and organizational activity stay separated.

  • Supply chain

    Signed builds, verified distribution, software bills of materials, dependency audits and fast patching.

  • You stay in control

    Data minimization, clear permissions, account export, deletion and retention settings, plus vulnerability disclosure and incident response.

The security program is built against NIST SP 800-53, NIST SP 800-207 zero-trust architecture, FIPS 140-3 validated cryptography where required, ISO/IEC 27001 and OWASP guidance, with independent penetration testing, source-code review and anonymity evaluations before every major release.
