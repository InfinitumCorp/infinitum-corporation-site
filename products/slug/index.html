SLUG™

SLUG™ is Infinitum's autonomous land drone: a low, quiet, tracked ground vehicle that carries loads, watches ground and extends the network where people should not have to go.

OVERVIEW
Military use only

SLUG™ stands just 45 cm tall, runs almost silently on electric tracks and keeps going for a full day on one battery. It is slow on purpose: steady enough to cross mud, sand, snow and rubble, low enough to stay out of sight and strong enough to carry 250 kg.

SLUG™ drives itself with onboard AI and needs no GPS. It follows a person, a route or a convoy, and every mile it drives feeds METATRON¹⁰⁰™ and, for authorized military users, ODIN°™.

There is no consumer or commercial edition. SLUG™ is sold only to authorized military and defense organizations as SLUG MIL™.

02 / MISSION MODULES

One drone, many jobs.

  • Cargo

    A flat, lockable deck for 250 kg of water, ammunition, tools, food or medical supplies, with a trailer hitch for more.

  • Casualty evacuation

    A low stretcher kit with restraints and shade lets SLUG™ carry an injured person out under cover.

  • Sensor mast

    A telescoping 3 m mast with day, thermal and radar sensors turns SLUG™ into a quiet observation post.

  • Network relay

    A FabriC++™ mesh node on the deck extends the network across valleys, tunnels and buildings where radios cannot reach.

  • Counter-drone

    In the military edition, a launcher for four AZRAIL MIL™ aircraft brings air defense to the front of a convoy or the edge of a site.

  • Patrol and inspection

    Scheduled routes around fences, pipelines and panels, with onboard AI flagging anything that changes.
