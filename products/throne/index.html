Own your compute.

OVERVIEW
AI computer line

Every #THRONE™ comes out of the box with the Infinitum ecosystem ready on first boot. Sign in once with your Cumulus://ID™ (CID) and your browser, AI agents, wallet, cloud, mail and messages are all connected before you open a single app.

AI runs on the machine itself, so it is fast, private and keeps working offline. And every #THRONE™ is secured from the chip up: keys that never leave the hardware, firmware verified against $Firmament Network™ at every boot, and physical privacy switches you can see.

03 / AI FROM THE CHIP UP

Smarter, safer hardware.

  • On-device AI

    Dedicated AI accelerators run SCALVS.ai™ models locally: private by default, instant and available offline.

  • THRONE Key

    A hardware root of trust holds your CID passkeys and $FIRM Wallet™ keys. They never leave the chip.

  • Attested firmware

    Firmware fingerprints are recorded on $Firmament Network™ and checked at every boot, so tampering is visible.

  • Privacy you can see

    Physical switches cut the camera and microphone on #THRONE Book™. No software can turn them back on.

  • One sign-in setup

    Sign in with CID and the machine sets up wallet, cloud, mail, AI and messaging in minutes.

  • Self-managing

    AI watches system health, predicts hardware failures and applies updates. Fleets are managed from the Cumulus Console™.
