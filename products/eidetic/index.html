ei·det·ic™

ei·det·ic™ is Infinitum's identity-intelligence platform: AI facial and identity matching with consent, purpose controls and a tamper-evident record of every search.

OVERVIEW
Consent-first identity intelligence

Identity matching is powerful, so ei·det·ic™ is built around control. Every search needs an authorized purpose, every match carries a confidence score, a person reviews every result, and every action is written to $Firmament Network™.

It adds permissioned identity context across the ecosystem: to [HRVST]™ captures, Spectre™ records and ODIN°™ operations, always within the permissions each user and organization has granted.

For defense organizations, ei·det·ic™ comes as ei·det·ic MIL™.

01 / HOW IT WORKS

How it works.

  1. 01 / AUTHORIZE

    Purpose first

    Role, purpose and legal basis checked before any search runs

  2. 02 / MATCH

    AI identity matching

    Facial and record matching with confidence scores

  3. 03 / REVIEW

    Human decision

    A trained reviewer confirms every result; no decisions on a match alone

  4. 04 / RECORD

    Audit everything

    Each search, match and decision written to $Firmament Network™

02 / WHAT MAKES IT SMARTER

Intelligence built in.

  • Consent-first enrollment

    People enroll through their Cumulus://ID™ (CID), see where their identity is used and can withdraw at any time.

  • Purpose-bound access

    Searches are limited to approved purposes, roles and datasets, and blocked when they fall outside them.

  • Explainable matches

    Each result shows its confidence, the evidence behind it and the alternatives considered.

  • Accuracy & bias testing

    Every release is tested for accuracy and demographic bias, and the results are published to customers.

  • Tamper-evident audit

    An unchangeable record of who searched for what, why and what happened next.

  • Isolated deployment

    Runs in sealed environments or on #THRONE Rack™ servers on premises, so biometric data never leaves the organization.
