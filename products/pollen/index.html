POLLEN™

POLLEN™ is a swarm of palm-sized autonomous aircraft that think together. Hundreds of drones share one mission, map large areas fast and keep working when individual aircraft drop out.

OVERVIEW
Military use only

POLLEN™ works as one system, not a collection of single drones. The aircraft coordinate among themselves, divide the work and adapt as conditions change.

Operators set the goal in METATRON¹⁰⁰™; the swarm decides how to achieve it. POLLEN™ launches from and returns to Flower™, relays its own network over FabriC++™ and streams what it senses into METATRON¹⁰⁰™.

There is no consumer or commercial edition. POLLEN™ is sold only to authorized military and defense organizations as POLLEN MIL™.

02 / WHAT MAKES IT SMARTER

Intelligence built in.
