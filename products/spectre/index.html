+SPCTR™ (Spectre) is Infinitum's AI-powered intelligent CRM. It researches every account for you, predicts what each relationship needs next, and puts AI agents to work on the busywork.

AI-powered · Blockchain-governed™ · Intelligent CRM

OVERVIEW
Intelligent CRM

Traditional CRMs are filing cabinets. They only know what your team remembers to type in, and they go stale the moment you stop updating them.

Spectre™ works the other way around. AI researches every account, keeps every record current on its own, reads the signals around each relationship and tells you what to do next. Your team spends its time with customers, not with data entry.

And it goes further than any ordinary CRM: Spectre™ can rehearse your next meeting before it happens, keep a record your customer co-signs, and close and pay out deals on its own.

01 / ONLY IN SPECTRE

What no ordinary CRM can do.

Spectre™ brings together three things Infinitum builds: AI that simulates the future, a blockchain both parties can trust, and identity people control themselves. Together they make a CRM that predicts, proves and pays.

  • Deal Rehearsal

    Before the meeting that matters, rehearse it. Spectre™ builds AI digital twins of the buying committee from your history and public information, then runs the conversation thousands of times on the prediction engine behind METATRON¹⁰⁰™. See which pitch lands, which objections come up and who needs convincing, before you walk in the room.

  • Shared Truth Ledger

    A CRM record that both sides can trust. Commitments, pricing, service terms and approvals are written to $Firmament Network™ and shared with your customer, so you and they hold the same signed version of the relationship. No more "that is not what we agreed."

  • Customer-Owned Profiles

    Contacts own their data. People share their profile and preferences from their Cumulus://ID™ (CID), choose exactly what you see and can withdraw it at any time. In return they can earn $SPCTR. You get accurate, consented data; they keep control.

  • Self-Executing Deals

    When a deal closes, the smart contract takes over: the agreement is signed, payment is collected through $FIRM Wallet™, and commissions and partner referral fees are paid out automatically, the moment the money arrives.

  • Competitive War Room

    Built on Infinitum's Corporate Cognitive Warfare practice. Spectre™ simulates how competitors are likely to respond to your pricing, launches and bids, so you can plan the counter-move first. Know the competition. Know yourself.
