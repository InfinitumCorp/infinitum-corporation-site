Flower™

Flower™ is the smart base station for POLLEN™. It launches, recovers, charges, updates and protects the swarm, and runs the AI that keeps every aircraft ready.

OVERVIEW
Military use only

POLLEN™ leaves the Flower™ and returns to the Flower™. Each station stores a fleet of palm-sized aircraft, launches them on command and brings them home to dock, charge and prepare for the next mission.

Flower™ is also a data and network hub. It offloads what the swarm captured to Cumulus://Cloud™, relays traffic over FabriC++™ and keeps every aircraft's software and maintenance records current.

There is no consumer or commercial edition. Flower™ is sold only to authorized military and defense organizations as Flower MIL™.

02 / WHAT MAKES IT SMARTER

Intelligence built in.

  • Autonomous launch & recovery

    Launches and docks aircraft without a pilot touching them.

  • Smart charging

    AI battery management predicts wear and rotates aircraft to keep the fleet healthy.

  • Self-maintenance

    Automatic health checks and firmware updates, with each update attested on $Firmament Network™.

  • Data hub

    Uploads captured data to Cumulus://Cloud™ the moment aircraft dock.

  • Network backhaul

    Built-in mesh node links the swarm to the rest of the network.

  • Fixed or portable

    Permanent installations or rugged portable cases, with environmental protection.
