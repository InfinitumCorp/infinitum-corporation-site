PRINCIPLES

How ODIN°™ stays in control

ONE OPERATING PICTURE

A single screen on the METATRON¹⁰⁰™ core, in a closed, accredited configuration. Every unit sees the same picture at the right classification level.

BLOCKCHAIN GOVERNANCE & CHAIN OF CUSTODY

A private, permissioned Firmament ledger enforces who may see, task or approve what, and records every view, change, approval and override. ODIN°™ has no public token and no data sale; mission data never enters the Infinitum Nexus™.

SOVEREIGN INFRASTRUCTURE

Runs in government regions, on deployable edge nodes, or fully air-gapped on Cumulus://Cloud™ and #THRONE MIL™ hardware. Mission data stays under the customer's control.

HUMAN COMMAND

Automation recommends and executes routine tasks. Any use of force requires an authorized human decision, under the rules of engagement and the law of armed conflict.
