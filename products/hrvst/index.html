[HRVST]™

[HRVST]™ (Harvest) turns what you see into value. Capture with camera glasses, let AI refine it into useful, privacy-filtered observations, and license them, or keep them private, on your terms.

Own your point of view.

OVERVIEW
Personal data exchange

Your perspective has value. Connect compatible glasses, choose a collection mode and decide what can leave your device. Harvest turns recordings into useful observations: a damaged sidewalk, a missing product on a shelf, a crop under stress or a first-person demonstration of a skilled task.

Before anything reaches the marketplace, AI filters out sensitive information: faces and license plates are blurred, private conversations removed and restricted locations excluded. You inspect, approve or delete every contribution.

03 / THE MARKETPLACE

Pay for usefulness, not volume.

  • Data tokens

    Machine-readable representations of approved observations.

  • Access tokens

    Permission to use a packet under specific license terms.

  • Harvest Credits

    Earned from purchases and spent on services or paid out.

  • Who buys

    Mapping providers, accessibility services, researchers, retailers and AI developers who need rights-cleared examples.

Harvest earns a fee on completed licenses, buyer subscriptions and enterprise tools; contributors receive a stated share of every purchase. The first market is permissioned fieldwork: maintenance teams, accessibility auditors and retail researchers.
