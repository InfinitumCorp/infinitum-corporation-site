$FIRM™ & $FIRM Wallet™

$FIRM™ is the nexus token of $Firmament Network™, and $FIRM Wallet™ is where it and every Infinitum token live: a standard self-custody wallet with a built-in decentralized exchange.

01 / THE $FIRM™ TOKEN
The nexus token

$FIRM™ is the principal token of $Firmament Network™ and the center of the Infinitum Nexus™. Every product token pairs with $FIRM™, so value earned in one product can move to any other, or out to USDC, without leaving the ecosystem.

$FIRM™ stays separate from the individual product tokens. Exchange listings follow each exchange's requirements and the regulations of each jurisdiction.

$FIRM™ WALLET
One wallet for every token

Every reward you earn across the Infinitum ecosystem lands in one place. $FIRM Wallet™ works like any standard crypto wallet: store, receive and send on Solana. It also has a decentralized exchange built in, so you can swap any Infinitum token for $FIRM™, USDC or another product's token without leaving the app.

You hold your own keys. Infinitum never takes custody of your funds.

02 / STANDARD + DEX

Everything a wallet should do. Plus a built-in exchange.

  • Standard wallet

    Store, receive and send SOL, USDC and every Infinitum token. Supports the SPL token standard on Solana.

  • Built-in DEX

    Swap through the Infinitum Nexus™: every product token pairs with $FIRM™ and USDC, so any token converts to any other in a few taps.

  • Rewards inbox

    Earnings from VEIL, [HRVST]™, Cumulus://Cloud™, FabriC++™ and every other product arrive here automatically, with a clear record of where each one came from.

03 / VEIL NEXUS

One sign-in for the whole ecosystem.

Through the VEIL Nexus, $FIRM Wallet™ connects to Cumulus://Cloud™, SCALVS.ai™, :\ARCHITECT\™, FabriC++™ and the rest of the Infinitum products. Pay for services, receive rewards and approve transactions from the same wallet.

  2. 02

05 / SECURITY

Your keys. Your device.

  • Self-custody

    Private keys are generated and stored on your device, protected by hardware-backed key storage where the device supports it.

  • Recovery

    A recovery phrase you control restores your wallet on a new device.

  • Clear approvals

    Every transaction and every product connection shows exactly what you are approving before you sign.
