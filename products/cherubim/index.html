CHERUBIM™

CHERUBIM™ is a family of three drones that give small units eyes on a situation first: CHERUBIM 1™ launches from a base dock the moment an alert comes in, CHERUBIM 2™ stands tethered overwatch for hours, and CHERUBIM 3™ flies inside buildings ahead of a team. Part of the DAEMON™ drone series.

Quick reaction · Persistent overwatch · Close-quarters reconnaissance

OVERVIEW
Military use only

The first minutes decide most engagements. CHERUBIM™ puts a camera over a threat before the quick-reaction force arrives, keeps it there as long as needed and sends it inside when soldiers would otherwise have to go in blind.

All three share one autonomy core, one secure video link and one flight record on $Firmament Network™. Video streams live to the operations center and to >_ARGUS MIL™ inside ODIN°™.

There is no consumer or commercial edition. CHERUBIM™ is sold only to authorized military and defense organizations as CHERUBIM MIL™.

01 / THREE MODELS

Three drones, three jobs.

  • CHERUBIM 1™

    Quick reaction. Lives in a weatherproof dock on a base or outpost and launches itself when a sensor or sentry raises an alert, arriving over the location in under two minutes with live video for the operations center and the responding force.

  • CHERUBIM 2™

    Tethered overwatch. Rises from a vehicle or transit case on a powered tether and stays up as long as needed, giving commanders a steady view over convoys, perimeters and checkpoints.

  • CHERUBIM 3™

    Indoor tactical. A small, caged drone that flies inside buildings, vehicles and tunnels without GPS, so a team can see and talk into a space before anyone enters.
