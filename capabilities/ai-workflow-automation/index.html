OVERVIEW

Most organizations already know which work is repetitive. The hard part is automating it safely: connecting the right systems, keeping people in the loop where decisions matter, and controlling what information AI can reach. Infinitum owns that project from first assessment to handover.

Every workflow we build has agreed human review points, permissioned access and controlled integrations. For military support organizations we implement approved administrative, logistics, maintenance and training workflows under the same controls.
