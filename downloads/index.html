Every Infinitum app is a direct download from this page. No request, no waiting list. Each app and platform has a permanent link, such as infinitumcorporation.ai/download/firm-suite/windows, that always points at the latest signed installer. Every installer is signed by Infinitum Corporation, and every release is recorded on $Firmament Network™, so you can check that what you install is exactly what we shipped.
