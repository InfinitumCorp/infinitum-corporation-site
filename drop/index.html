    Questions

    Before you register

    Does it cost anything?

    No. Registration is free and no purchase is needed to receive the drop. Claiming only costs the usual Solana network fee, a fraction of a cent.

    Why Solana?

    Every Infinitum token, $FIRM included, is issued on Solana: fast settlement and sub-cent fees for high-volume product rewards, all in one wallet.

    Can I change my tokens after registering?

    Yes, until the snapshot. Register the same address again with new picks and your early-viewer number stays the same.

    How is my allocation calculated?

    Each token reserves a drop pool. It is split across every registered wallet that picked that token, weighted by tier multiplier. Pool sizes are published with the final terms on 15 December.

    Where do I claim?

    In $FIRM Wallet™ or any Solana wallet, from 00:00 UTC on 1 January 2027 until the claim window closes on 31 March.

    Will anyone message me about the drop?

    No. Infinitum will never message you first, ask for a seed phrase, or ask you to send tokens to receive tokens. Treat any message like that as a scam and report it.

    Tokens distributed in the Genesis Drop are utility tokens for use within Infinitum products. They are not an investment, carry no promise of value or return, and are not offered where prohibited. Pools, tiers and dates are subject to final terms published before the snapshot.
